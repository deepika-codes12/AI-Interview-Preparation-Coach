# STAR Answer Bank — 10 Templates

**Replace every bracketed section with a true example. Do not present hypothetical events as personal experience.**

1. **Problem-solving**
   - Situation: [real problem in class/project]
   - Task: [your responsibility]
   - Action: I [steps you actually took]
   - Result: [real outcome or lesson]

2. **Teamwork**
   - Situation: [real team assignment]
   - Task: [your role]
   - Action: [how you coordinated or resolved a blocker]
   - Result: [actual result]

3. **Explaining something technical**
   - Situation: [real situation]
   - Task: [who needed help]
   - Action: [simple explanation/example you used]
   - Result: [what changed]

4. **Mistake and correction**
   - Situation: [real mistake]
   - Task: [what needed correcting]
   - Action: [how you acknowledged and fixed it]
   - Result: [lesson/prevention step]

5. **Managing deadlines**
   - Situation: [overlapping real deadlines]
   - Task: [deliverables]
   - Action: [prioritisation and tracking method]
   - Result: [actual outcome]

6. **Learning a tool quickly**
   - Situation: [tool/topic]
   - Task: [what you needed to learn]
   - Action: [documentation/practice steps]
   - Result: [what you can now do]

7. **Handling a frustrated user (hypothetical)**
   - Listen, acknowledge impact, clarify symptoms, troubleshoot safely, explain progress, document actions, and escalate when appropriate.

8. **Unresolved technical issue (hypothetical)**
   - Review steps, collect error details, check approved knowledge base, avoid risky changes, document attempted fixes, consult senior support and escalate.

9. **Conflicting priorities**
   - Clarify deadlines and business impact; communicate with the responsible lead; agree the order; update ticket/status notes.

10. **Why should we hire you as a fresher?**
    - Link genuine learning ability, foundational knowledge, patience, careful documentation and willingness to follow procedures. Only mention skills you can demonstrate.
