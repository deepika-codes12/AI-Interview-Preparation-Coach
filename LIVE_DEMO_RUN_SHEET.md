# Live Demo Run Sheet (5–7 minutes)

1. **Introduce the problem (30 sec):** Freshers need role-specific practice and feedback.
2. **Show research (60 sec):** Open `Research_Summary.md` and `Verification_Log.csv`. Point out the difference between employer-supported duties and AI-generated practice questions.
3. **Demonstrate prompt engineering (60 sec):** Open `Prompt_Library.md`; run the IT Support prompt and ask one question at a time.
4. **Demonstrate multimodal image analysis (60–90 sec):** Open `Multimodal_IT_Support_Sample.png`. Ask the AI to read the screenshot, separate visible facts from possible causes, and suggest safe L1 troubleshooting. Then show `Multimodal_Demo_Analysis.md`. The image is synthetic and labelled as a training sample.
5. **Demonstrate evaluation (60 sec):** Show one mock answer, the rubric and `Mock_Interview_Tracker.xlsx`. Explain formula-based averages and conditional formatting.
6. **Show results (60 sec):** Explain 76.6 vs 97.4, the +20.8 indicative difference, and separate Gemini 99.8 score.
7. **Ethics and limitations (30 sec):** AI scores are subjective; generated questions are not employer questions; do not share private information or invent experiences.

Do not claim an actual network was diagnosed or fixed. The multimodal demonstration is image interpretation of a synthetic training screenshot.
