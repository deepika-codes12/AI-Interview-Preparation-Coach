# Prompt Library

## 1. Business Analyst research prompt
Act as a career research assistant for fresh graduates in India preparing for entry-level Business Analyst interviews. Research current entry-level Business Analyst job descriptions from employer career websites and reputable sources. Prepare responsibilities, skills grouped by area, 25 suggested questions, common fresher mistakes, source table with publisher/date/direct URL, distinction between sourced requirements and suggested questions, conflicts/outdated information/unsupported claims. Do not invent citations, employer requirements, salaries, statistics or company-specific interview questions. Prioritize India-based junior roles and state research date and limitations.

## 2. QA Tester research prompt
Act as a career research assistant for fresh graduates in India preparing for entry-level QA Tester / Software Testing interviews. Research current India-based job descriptions, prioritizing official employer career pages and junior roles. Provide responsibilities, grouped skills, 25 suggested questions, common mistakes, source table with dates and direct URLs, fresher-suitable versus experience-heavy role distinctions, limitations, and no invented citations or employer questions.

## 3. IT Support mock-interview prompt
Act as a realistic interviewer for an entry-level IT Support / L1 Service Desk role in India. Ask one question at a time. Conduct HR (5), basic IT/troubleshooting (10), and customer-support/behavioural (5) rounds. Do not show model answers before the candidate responds. Include Windows, Wi-Fi/networking, password resets, software issues, ticket documentation, prioritization and escalation. After each answer, give feedback and a truthful improved answer. Score relevance (25), technical accuracy (25), clarity (20), practical reasoning (15), structure (15). Explain scores, track them, and label scores as AI estimates, not official results.

## 4. Second-model verification prompt (use only if actually run)
Review the following claim and source excerpt. Separate (a) what the source directly supports, (b) reasonable inference, and (c) unsupported statements. Do not use outside facts unless clearly labelled. Identify missing context, outdated details, and what primary source would be needed. Claim: [paste claim]. Source excerpt and URL: [paste excerpt and URL].

## 5. Answer feedback prompt
Evaluate my answer for a fresher-level [ROLE] interview using this fixed rubric: relevance 25, accuracy 25, clarity 20, practical reasoning 15, structure 15. Give a reason for each score, one priority improvement, and a revised answer that does not invent my experience. Treat scores as coaching estimates, not employer-certified results.
