# One-Page Interview Checklist (Paste into Canva)

## BEFORE THE INTERVIEW
☐ Read the job description and identify the main skills.
☐ Prepare a 60-second introduction.
☐ Prepare 3 genuine STAR examples from college/projects.
☐ Check audio, internet, and a quiet environment.

## DURING THE INTERVIEW
☐ Listen to the whole question; ask a clarifying question if needed.
☐ Answer directly, then explain your reasoning.
☐ Use STAR for behavioural questions.
☐ Be honest about what you know and what you are still learning.

## ROLE-SPECIFIC REMINDERS
**IT SUPPORT:** Clarify symptoms and impact → check simple causes → troubleshoot safely step by step → keep user informed → document actions → escalate when appropriate.

**BUSINESS ANALYST:** Clarify business goal and stakeholders → distinguish requirement from solution → define acceptance criteria → consider scope and traceability.

**QA TESTER:** Understand expected behaviour → cover positive, negative and boundary cases → record steps, expected vs actual result, environment and evidence.

## RESPONSIBLE AI
☐ Verify technical facts and source claims.
☐ Do not memorise unverified AI answers.
☐ Never invent experience, qualifications or certifications.
☐ Protect personal and confidential data.

## AFTER
☐ Record feedback and scores.
☐ Pick three improvement areas.
☐ Practise weak topics and update your answer bank.
